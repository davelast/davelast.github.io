A hook earns a moment. The rest of the ad has to make that moment worthwhile: a relevant offer, a believable argument, and an obvious next step.

Agree on one primary business outcome. Attention, click-through, and watch time help locate a problem; they do not establish profitable acquisition.

Five questions before anything ships.

  1. Who is this for, and what situation makes it relevant now?
  2. What single promise should they take away?
  3. What gives them a reason to believe it?
  4. Will they recognize the product and understand the next step?
  5. What will we learn, and what result would change our decision?
