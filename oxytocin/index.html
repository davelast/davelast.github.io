The science / 02

A familiar hormone.
A new conversation.

Oxytocin is a nine-amino-acid hormone involved in social bonding and stress regulation. Researchers are exploring a topical formulation for mood and sleep.

Questions / 05

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is this a supplement?

No. The source product is a compounded prescription medication. It is not FDA-approved for safety, efficacy, or quality.

What does the research tell us?

The early report tracks changes in self-reported outcomes. An uncontrolled study can identify signals for further research, but cannot separate a treatment effect from expectations or other changes.

Who should discuss safety with a clinician?

Everyone considering treatment. Maximus advises against use during pregnancy and flags heart failure, kidney disease, low sodium, and fluid-balance conditions for medical review. This is not a complete safety list.

How do I use the cream?

Follow your prescribing clinician’s instructions. The source page contains differing application descriptions, so this concept does not provide a dose or application protocol.

Can I buy it here?

No. This is an independent concept homepage. You can review the source product at Maximus.
