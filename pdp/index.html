What is Solius Pro cleared for?

Solius describes its OTC panel as FDA-cleared to stimulate vitamin D production in adults 22 and older. Read the clearance information.

Is a membership required?

Yes. Activation requires a membership. Solius lists monthly and annual plans, plus a limited founding offer. Check the official shop for availability.

How is it different from red light?

Solius uses UVB; red-light devices use a different part of the light spectrum. They are different technologies.

Is UVB exposure risk-free?

No. Solius uses personalized dosing and safety features to reduce risk, but all UV exposure carries some risk. Follow the user manual, including protective eyewear requirements.
